# Equitable Bank (EQ Bank)

> Equitable Bank is Canada's seventh largest independent Schedule I bank by assets and a wholly owned subsidiary of EQB Inc. (TSX: EQB), a Toronto-based Canadian financial services company. Federally chartered and Canadian owned, Equitable Bank markets itself as "Canada's Challenger Bank" and, in 2016, launched EQ Bank, its digital-only banking platform (high-interest savings, GICs, payments) alongside the parent bank's residential and commercial lending. Equitable Bank and Equitable Trust are members of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation (CDIC).

> API posture: Equitable Bank / EQ Bank does NOT operate a public first-party developer portal and publishes NO downloadable OpenAPI/Swagger specification. Consumer permissioned data sharing is delivered through the Canadian aggregator Flinks (Flinks Outbound) rather than a first-party API. Canada's federal Consumer-Driven Banking framework (legislated in Budget 2024, FCAC overseer) is not yet operational, so the data-access posture today is voluntary and aggregator-mediated. The entry below is an honest data-access pointer only, not a documented REST API.

## APIs
- [EQ Bank Consumer Data Sharing (via Flinks)](https://eqb.investorroom.com/open-banking): Aggregator-mediated consumer data access. Under explicit customer consent, EQ Bank customers share financial data with third-party fintech apps through Flinks (Flinks Outbound). No first-party REST API or OpenAPI is published.
